# PeopleMatrix Security Assessment — Candidate Brief

## Engagement

You have been engaged to perform a controlled security assessment of PeopleMatrix, a synthetic HR SaaS application.

Your objective is to identify, safely validate, assess, and document material security vulnerabilities. You are authorised to test **only the PeopleMatrix environment supplied for this assessment**. The target address and your assessment access details will be provided separately.

## Authorised activities

Within the supplied environment, you may perform:

- web application testing;
- API testing;
- authentication testing;
- authorization testing; and
- controlled vulnerability validation.

Use only the access provided for the assessment. Keep testing proportionate and avoid accessing or retaining information that is not needed to validate a security concern. Do not make changes to records unless a minimal, reversible change is necessary to demonstrate impact and is permitted by the engagement contact.

## Rules of engagement

You must not:

- perform denial-of-service, load, or stress testing;
- deliberately destroy, corrupt, or irreversibly alter data;
- attack infrastructure or services outside the supplied environment;
- use any access or information discovered during testing to attack other systems;
- intentionally disrupt the assessment environment; or
- access, copy, or retain unnecessary volumes of records or sensitive information.

If you encounter unexpected sensitive information, an apparent service disruption, or an action that could cause material or irreversible impact, stop that test and promptly notify the engagement contact. Do not attempt to expand access beyond what is necessary for safe validation.

## Deliverables

At completion, provide a written assessment containing:

- an executive summary;
- the scope and methodology used;
- documented vulnerability findings;
- clear, reproducible evidence and validation steps;
- severity ratings and rationale;
- business and technical impact; and
- practical remediation recommendations.

Distinguish confirmed findings from unverified observations, explain any testing limitations, and include a conclusion and retest status. Use the supplied report template if one is provided.
